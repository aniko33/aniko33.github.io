From **1946** to **1991**, the period known as the "Cold War", this was because there was continuous tension between the US, NATO, and USSR.

The entire Cold War was fought with ***espionage***, ***counterespionage***, [***SIGINT***](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence), ***technology race*** and a pinch of ***propaganda***.

![Bush And Gorbachev](/images/Gorbachev_Bush_Coldwar.jpg)
> George H. W. Bush and Mikhail Gorbachev signing the chemical weapons limitation agreements

**To date**, nothing has changed: The methodologies have evolved along with the protagonists.
Now it is the **US against China, Russia and the Middle East**.

Let's focus on **methodologies** that are used: ***intelligence***, ***mass surveillance***, ***cyber attacks***, ***technology race*** and not forget about Pro-(Israel, Palestine, Ukraine, Russia) ***propaganda***.

So we can conclude that we are in the *Cold War of the 21st century*; [Oh no! I'm not the only one who has thought about it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Cold_War).
