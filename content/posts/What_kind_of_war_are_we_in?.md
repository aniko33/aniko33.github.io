From **1946** to **1991**, the period known as the "Cold War", this was because there was continuous tension between the US, NATO, and USSR.

The entire Cold War was fought with ***espionage***, ***counterespionage***, [***SIGINT***](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence), ***technology race*** and a pinch of ***propaganda***.

>![Bush And Gorbachev](/images/Gorbachev_Bush_Coldwar.jpg)

> George H. W. Bush and Mikhail Gorbachev signing the chemical weapons limitation agreements

**To date**, nothing has changed: The methodologies have evolved along with the protagonists.
Now it is the **US against China, Russia and the Middle East**.

Let's focus on **methodologies** that are used today: ***intelligence***, ***mass surveillance***, ***cyber attacks***, ***technology race*** and not forget about Pro-(Israel, Palestine, Ukraine, Russia) ***propaganda***.

So we can conclude that we are in the *Cold War of the 21st century*; [Oh no! I'm not the only one who has thought about it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Cold_War).

> ![F-35_Lightning_II](/images/F-35_Lightning_II.jpg)

> *F-35 Lightning II*: High-tech aircraft used by NATO allies

# The intelligence

> *"Intelligence is the field of data gathering, evaluation, assessing and analysis and involves branch of cyber security, cryptology, espionage and others"*

In the years of the **Cold War I**, **the spies were soldiers** trained to endure heavy interrogation sessions. They would kill themselves with cyanide if they were detected on a mission.
The role of **spies is very important to gain secrets of another country**, and that country gives a advantage in tactical view.

Today the role of spy is pretty similar but now is applied for **anti-terrorism**, **direct conflicts** (like Palestine and Israel) and to **caught illegal drug trade**.

> ![Aquiline](/images/Aquiline.jpg)

> *Project AQUILINE*: Was a secret 1960s ***CIA*** program to develop an unmanned aerial reconnaissance vehicle

**Where is the evolution?** The evolution is the cyber security field, now ***APT groups*** (*advanced persistent threat*) can [*pwn*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet#Owned_and_pwned) entire network via ***vulnerabilities***, ***social engineering*** or with ***physical a attack***.

## What is a APT

> *"An APT is a stealthy threat actor, typically a state or state-sponsored group, which gains unauthorized access to a computer network and remains undetected for an extended period."*
